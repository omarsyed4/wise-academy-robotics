Questions to ask

Innovation Project
  1. Why did you pick this problem?
  2. Who did you talk to outside your team, and what did they tell you?
  3. What already exists, and how is yours different?
  4. What did you change after feedback?
  5. Who would actually use this?
  6. What was the hardest part, and what would you do next?
Robot Design
  1. Why those missions and not others?
  2. Show me an attachment. Why is it built that way?
  3. Which sensors do you use, and why that one?
  4. What broke, and what did you change?
  5. How many times did you test this run?
  6. Who wrote this part of the code?
