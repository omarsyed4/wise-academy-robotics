Solution 2 • A government law

HOW THE LAW WORKS

Step 1

A construction worker wants to clear a forest to build a new neighborhood.

Step 2

Environmental regulations require buying two separate pieces of land:

ALand A: Cleared to build the new neighborhood.
BLand B: Protected and used to plant new trees to replace the old.
Step 3

The worker buys both plots, builds the neighborhood on Land A, and plants the replacement forest on Land B.
