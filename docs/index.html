COACH CALL

Show how we learned

The judges want to hear the students' process: what we tried, what happened, what we changed and what we learned. A clear drawing and an honest explanation can be useful even while the project is unfinished.

Own the explanation

Use Identify → Design → Create → Iterate → Communicate as prompts. Practise answering from the rubric, beyond memorising a script. Students handle questions; coaches and observing parents stay silent.

Make it work on paper

Bring readable drawings, code screenshots and a brief evidence pack. No venue TV or power is promised. Digital material is optional, offline and charged; practise the same explanation with it switched off.

Name the six values

Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork and Fun. Each student should be ready with a real team example. Support one another while presenting; the behaviour matters too.

Turn setbacks into evidence

M8's rightward miss and alignment changes already tell a useful story. Keep the failed version, the change and the retest result. Record team/expert conversations as: who, when, advice, what we changed, result.

Practise a legal match

No hand transfers between homes, no switching sides and no hand-resetting field models during a match. Same-side technician swaps must keep at most two at a home. Only students handle robots and programming; leave computers/tablets in the pit.

Check the field

Choose and verify the M13–15 model/dock layout before the start. The call described a queue selection sheet; confirm the procedure with our event. Check no-equipment mission conditions before trying detached mechanisms.

14 October official update: prepare for both taped and untaped trees on M8/M9. Autonomous use of the built-in latch to release interchangeable dock models is allowed. Read the current Challenge Updates.

Lucky Leaves

First leaf off the nest 10, second leaf off with the katydid still in place plus 20.

Not in plan

The only mission that can score zero after you do the work. The leaves are randomised and you are not told where the katydid starts. If it ends up completely outside the leaf habitat, the whole mission scores nothing. Late season at the earliest.

Reaching Roots

Partially extended 10, or completely extended 20.

RedCoded

There is no bonus here. The Red game plan has it as 10 plus a 10 bonus. It scores 10 or 20, never both. Aiming for a bonus that does not exist means building the wrong attachment.

Biocentric Architecture

Canopy raised 10, skylight in 10, compost hatch open 10, plus a 10 environmental bonus.

BlueTried 9 Oct

Blue tried it on 9 October; the window part was not working in that record. They tried making the robot turn, and it didn't work. Not in the plan or the run order as of 9 October.

Do all three actions and the dock position stops mattering, because the bonus scores from whichever dock the model sits on. Mohamed and Ammar both logged this as 60 points. It is 40.

PRE20

Equipment inspection

Robot and all equipment inside one launch area, under 12 inches.

Drill

Worth the same as Window to the Past and takes thirty seconds to practise. The pack down should be identical every single time.

ALL50

Precision tokens

Six at the start. Six or five left both pay 50.

Drill

The first interruption outside home still loses a token; five remaining retains 50 points. The second costs 15, a substantial loss against a 20-point mission. After that: 4 tokens is 35, 3 is 25, 2 is 15, 1 is 10. Wait the extra two seconds for the robot to come home.
